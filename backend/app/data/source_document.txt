Narrative detail:
- Revenue increased 21.6% to €836,991 (2024: €688,317) driven by client acquisition and UK market expansion.
- Gross margin expanded to 77.5% from 62.8%, reflecting improved operational efficiency and reductions in cost of sales.
- Administrative expenses decreased by €274,795 (18%) to €1,286,058, primarily through staff cost optimisation.
- Operating loss in 2025 decreased 44% to €633,694 (2024: €1,130,729).
- Net loss after tax decreased 46% to €590,256 (2024: €1,098,095).
- Net liabilities of €15,575 as at 30 June 2025 vs net assets of €574,681 as at 30 June 2024, due to accumulated losses.
- Trade debtors reduced from €142,361 (30 June 2024) to €66,990 (30 June 2025), a reduction of €51,727.
- Creditors falling due within one year increased by €153,768: trade creditors increased from €26,768 (30 June 2024) to €55,246 (30 June 2025); balance from Directors' loans in the period.
- Creditors falling due after more than one year increased by €83,655 following drawdown of a new bank loan.
- Cash and cash equivalents decreased to €140,135 (30 June 2025) from €424,639 (30 June 2024).
- Net cash used in operating activities decreased 68% from €1,166,697 (2024) to €374,820 (2025), due to reduced operating losses and positive working capital movements.
- A new €100,000 SBCI-backed term loan was secured during the period, providing positive net cash generation from financing activities.
- Net cash used in investing activities was minimal in the period (capex of €3,451 in FY2025, €33,472 in FY2024) — consistent with an asset-light software business with negligible depreciable fixed-asset base; no separate depreciation & amortisation figure disclosed in the summary financial information.
- R&D expenditure: FY2025 approximately 17% of revenue; FY2024 approximately 22% of revenue.
- Company anticipates R&D as a percentage of revenue will decline as it scales.
- Directors loans: Anthony Childs (€100,000) and Gerard Keenan (€70,000) received as short-term working capital loans in instalments from March 2025; both loans were fully repaid in October 2025 (so not outstanding at year end / admission).
- Company is well capitalised for organic growth through the two financial years ending 30 June 2027, following completion of the 2025 Private Placement (€1.1m gross proceeds, €13.1m post-money enterprise valuation, implied share price €5.126).
- Company anticipates becoming EBITDA positive during FY2028.

KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (Section 7.3):
- Primary KPI: Revenue Growth. Target: CAGR of no less than 50% through FY2030 (Senus 2030 strategy), vs. €836,991 FY2025 revenue base.
- Enterprise Customers: 36 Enterprise customers in FY2025. Target: grow to more than 100 Enterprise customers by FY2030.
- Average Annual Contract Value (ACV) with Enterprise Customers FY2025: €12,309 (Senus SOIL), €21,524 (Senus TERRAIN), €58,900 (Senus ERA). Target: average ACV across Enterprise customers to exceed €50,000 by FY2030.
- Geographic mix: Ireland accounted for approximately 78% of FY2025 revenue (equivalently ~22% of revenue came from outside Ireland in the twelve months to 30 June 2025, compared to less than 5% in the twelve months to 30 June 2024). Target: Ireland to account for less than 50% of revenue by FY2030.
- Profitability KPI: Target EBITDA positive during FY2028.
